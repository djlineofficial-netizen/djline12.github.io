About

Sound design meets dancefloor energy.

DJ Line is a full-time Atlanta DJ and co-founder of ButterflyZ Collective — a DJ ecosystem platform building community, content, and live experiences across the city.

With a B.F.A. in Sound Design from SCAD Atlanta and over five years behind the decks, she brings technical precision and crowd-read instincts to every set. Whether it's a branded corporate event or an intimate underground session, the energy is always intentional.
